Our Authentic Homemade Snacks Collection in Pune

Explore our signature lineup of authentic Indian savories prepared fresh on order with premium ingredients and hand-selected spices.

1. Salted Shankarpali (नमकीन शंकरपाळी)

Crispy, hand-rolled savory diamonds kneaded in high-grade refined rice bran oil with mild sea salt and pure wheat flour. Uses traditional "Moyan" dough-kneading technique to create micro-layers that expand upon frying for a delicate melt-in-the-mouth texture. Contains zero soda, zero artificial preservatives, and zero vanaspati. Available in 250g (₹90), 500g (₹170), and 1kg (₹330) packs.

2. Tangy Shankarpali (तिखट-मीठ शंकरपाळी)

A sweet, sour, and spicy twist on classic shankarpali. Hand-rolled wheat dough coated with hand-ground amchur powder, red chili, organic sugar, and chat masala. Deep-fried in refined rice bran oil for an addictive, tangy flavor profile. Available in 250g (₹100), 500g (₹190), and 1kg (₹370) packs.

3. Premium Roasted Poha Chivda (विशेष पोहा चिवडा)

Crispy thin roasted rice flakes (poha) tossed with premium roasted cashews, peanuts, dry coconut slices, green chillies, curry leaves, and Vidya Dandekar's secret Pune spice mix. Completely oil-free roasted base snack, perfect for daily tea-time diet. Available in 250g (₹110), 500g (₹210), and 1kg (₹400) packs.

The Swadam Foods Brand Story & Recipe Tradition

Founded by Vidya Dandekar in Dhayari, Pune, Swadam Foods is dedicated to preserving the rich culinary heritage of authentic Maharashtrian tea-time snacks and festive faral boxes. What started as handcrafted delicacies for family gatherings and festival celebrations has grown into a beloved local brand trusted by hundreds of Pune families.

Vidya Dandekar shares: "As a homemaker, I understand that food is about safety, trust, and sharing absolute warmth. I use only pure wheat flour, 100% refined rice bran oil, and hand-ground whole spices. We never add chemical preservatives, artificial food colorings, or baking soda."

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are Swadam Foods snacks prepared fresh on order?

Yes, all items including Salted Shankarpali, Tangy Shankarpali, and Special Poha Chivda are prepared fresh in small batches upon receiving your order.

What oil is used for cooking?

We strictly use 100% high-grade refined rice bran oil, which is heart-healthy, non-greasy, and rich in natural antioxidants. We do not use vanaspati or artificial butter.

What is the shelf life of the snacks?

When stored in an airtight container in a dry place, Poha Chivda stays crispy for 4 to 6 weeks, while Shankarpali remains fresh for 3 to 4 weeks.

Do you accept bulk orders for weddings, corporate gifting, and festivals in Pune?

Yes! We specialize in bulk orders for wedding faral boxes, corporate gifting, birthdays, Diwali, and Ganesh Chaturthi celebrations across Dhayari, Sinhagad Road, and all Pune localities.
